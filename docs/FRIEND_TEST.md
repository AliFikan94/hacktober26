# 10-minute friend test

**Before he sits down:** run `start.bat`, check the header pill shows a model, click it and press **Test the students** (all ✓).

**Say to him:** "Try to learn one small thing and explain it to the three students. Say out loud whatever you're thinking, including what annoys you. You can't break it."

**Watch silently. Don't help.** Note: where does he hesitate? What does he try to click? Does he use the mic or type?

**Ask after (write his words down exactly, ask permission to quote him):**
1. In one sentence, what is this?
2. Did the students' questions help you understand better, or annoy you? Which student?
3. Did you ever not know what to do next?
4. Would you use this again tomorrow after work? Why or why not?
5. What's the one thing you'd change?
6. Was talking easier than typing for you?

**Save:** a screenshot of his session (with his OK), and 2 or 3 direct quotes.
